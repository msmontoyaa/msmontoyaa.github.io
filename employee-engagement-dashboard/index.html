Employee Engagement & Attrition Dashboard

Survey ratings, attrition and an early-warning risk score for 1,470 employees. Built by Maria Montoya-Chang on IBM's fictional HR Analytics dataset. See the analysis on GitHub ↗

How employees rate each area

Share of employees giving each rating, from 1 (lowest) to 4 (highest). Low ratings sit left of the line.

Who left, by rating

Share of employees who left at each rating. The dashed line is the overall rate for the current selection.

Attrition and engagement by role

Engagement is the average of the five survey ratings (1 to 4). Click a heading to sort.

Watch list: current employees with the highest risk

Employees still at the company, ranked by a cross-validated risk score from the analysis. In practice a list like this would only be used to offer support, such as a check-in with their manager.
